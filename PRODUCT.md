# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual geography players, students, teachers, and puzzle fans using a desktop or mobile browser. Their primary task is to reconstruct a country or regional border from memory, understand the scoring feedback, and repeat or share the result without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

Country Draw turns geographic recall into a short playable loop on a real map. Success means the map is geographically trustworthy, drawing and navigation feel predictable, scoring explains the error clearly, and a completed result is easy to repeat or share.

## Brand Personality

Playful, direct, and trustworthy. The game can be expressive at the moment of scoring, but controls and explanations should remain clear and practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid approximate or misleading map shapes, opaque scoring, marketing-first layouts, decorative dashboards, excessive modal steps, and sharing flows that disguise copying as sharing.

## Design Principles

1. Put the playable map and primary action before explanation.
2. Make every score diagnostic through matched, missed, and extra areas.
3. Use familiar controls and explicit action labels.
4. Preserve geographic accuracy across countries, islands, and map projections.
5. Make successful results easy to repeat and visually worth sharing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, touch targets of at least 40 CSS pixels, reduced-motion support, and labels or percentages in addition to result colors.
